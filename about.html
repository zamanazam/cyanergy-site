About the company

Real-time insight for cleaner, more efficient fleets

Cyanergy is a Houston-based company specializing in emissions monitoring and engine optimization for shipping and oil and gas companies. By leveraging Internet of Things (IoT) and machine learning technologies, we help ships, drilling rigs, and other industrial equipment track emissions in real time — so teams can monitor, report, and mitigate their carbon footprint more effectively.

Beyond compliance, Cyanergy’s solutions are designed to optimize operations and reduce costs. By coupling real-time data with actionable insight into engine performance, we help clients improve both sustainability and profitability. Our approach integrates with onboard sensors, minimizing capital investment while yielding tangible results.

With over a decade of experience in environmental technology innovations, Cyanergy has become a trusted partner for industries seeking expert solutions in carbon emission management and IoT-driven monitoring systems.

About the founders

Leadership shaped by regulatory change

At Cyanergy, our founders have been at the forefront of integrating IoT technology into emissions management — developing scalable solutions that help industries achieve environmental goals with confidence.

With over 25 years of C-suite experience in major public companies, our leadership understands the transformative power of regulatory shifts. We believe emerging emissions requirements will drive greater accountability — comparable in impact to frameworks like Sarbanes-Oxley (SOX) or EU Transparency certifications — and accelerate adoption of technology that meets sustainability targets.

Cyanergy is committed to delivering high ROI by going beyond compliance. Our solutions help ensure regulatory adherence while driving cost savings: automating manual calculations and reporting, optimizing engine performance, lowering fuel consumption, and reducing emissions.
